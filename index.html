What NZ Desk does

Most investors can access filings, consensus and broker models. NZ Desk is built around what is harder to export: local context, corporate history, shareholder dynamics, management behaviour and an independent view of what actually matters.

01

Situation Monitor

A concise weekly institutional briefing on the corporate developments, capital raisings, M&A, governance changes and unusual situations worth paying attention to.

02

Rapid Reaction

Short, opinionated notes after important announcements. Not “what happened?” but “what changed, what matters, and what is the market missing?”

03

Deep Dives

Longer research on companies, sectors and situations: capital allocation, ownership, incentives, competitive structure, management records and valuation.

04

Models & data

Auditable models, ownership maps, transaction histories, peer work and company data where they make the research better — not spreadsheets for decoration.

05

Company dossiers

Living files on management, boards, major shareholders, acquisitions, incentives, controversies and previous strategic promises.

06

Direct access

For institutional tiers: structured calls and tightly bounded research requests when a live NZ situation suddenly matters to your portfolio.

Independence is part of the product

The value of NZ Desk depends on clients believing the work is written for them, not for issuers, brokers, sponsors or corporate-finance relationships.

NZ Desk will not

Accept issuer-paid equity research. It will not earn investment-banking fees, capital-raising fees or execution commissions. Institutional research is sponsor-free.

NZ Desk will

Disclose meaningful conflicts, maintain personal-account dealing and MNPI policies, and keep commercial sponsorship elsewhere in EBR clearly separated from institutional research.

Questions investors ask us

NZ Desk is most useful when the data is available but the context is not.

How is this different from broker research?

Broker research is strong at forecasts, models and corporate access. NZ Desk is deliberately complementary: corporate history, ownership, capital allocation, management behaviour, special situations and an independent local interpretation without an execution incentive.

Do you cover every NZX company?

No. Scarcity is deliberate. Coverage focuses on companies and situations where local knowledge, corporate memory and independent judgement can change the investment conclusion.

Can clients request bespoke work?

Institutional and Strategic clients receive a bounded number of research credits. Larger custom diligence assignments are scoped and priced separately.

Is NZ Desk investment advice?

The service is intended for professional/wholesale clients. Final legal and regulatory disclosures should be confirmed before deployment and cross-border distribution.
