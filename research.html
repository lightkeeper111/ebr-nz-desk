Research

Research that starts where the announcement ends.

NZ Desk is designed to answer the second-order questions: who benefits, what changed, what management has promised before, which shareholders matter, and whether the market is framing the situation correctly.

A

Corporate memory

Management histories, prior strategies, acquisitions, divestments, promises and outcomes tracked over time.

B

Ownership

Major shareholders, control dynamics, activist potential, corporate actions and the incentives sitting behind a register.

C

Capital allocation

The bridge from accounting results to what management actually did with shareholders’ money.

D

Management

Compensation, turnover, track records, operating history and the qualitative evidence that often gets flattened out of consensus.

E

Special situations

M&A, capital raisings, restructurings, shareholder disputes and moments where local detail matters disproportionately.

F

Culture & demand

For consumer companies, financial analysis combined with the cultural and competitive changes that often lead the reported numbers.
