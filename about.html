About

Independent judgement, with a long memory.

EBR NZ Desk is led by Eden Bradfield and built for investment teams that need New Zealand context without maintaining a dedicated local analyst.

Eden Bradfield

Investor, researcher and business columnist.

Eden Bradfield has worked in New Zealand investment management and financial research and now writes regular corporate and markets columns for BusinessDesk, with work carried by the NZ Herald. He also appears on Herald NOW Business with Garth Bray and publishes independent analysis through Eden Bradfield Research.

The work tends to focus on management performance, capital allocation, incentives, ownership, M&A, governance and situations where the conventional market framing looks incomplete.

NZ Desk takes that approach and turns it into a structured institutional product: recurring monitoring, deeper research, data and direct access for professional investors.

The operating principle

New Zealand is not an information-free market. It is an information-rich market in which much of the useful context is fragmented across filings, interviews, local history, shareholder registers, prior management decisions and relationships that are obvious to people who follow the market closely but opaque to offshore investors.

NZ Desk exists to organise that context and apply judgement to it.

Editorial and commercial separation. EBR may have commercial sponsors elsewhere in the public publishing business. NZ Desk institutional research is sponsor-free. Sponsors have no approval rights over research coverage, conclusions or distribution.
